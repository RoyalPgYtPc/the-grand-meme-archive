A preservation project, not a gallery.

The Great Meme Archive is an independent archival project, operated as an official part of r/MemeExchangeCommunism — the internet's home for free memes, founded May 5, 2025 after a rival community banned image replies in comments.

Where the main community site is about who's in it — the mods, the elections, MemeCoin — the Archive exists to answer a different question: what did this community actually post, and can we still find it?

The core idea

The meme doesn't disappear just because the image isn't currently hosted.

GitHub Pages — where the archive's public media lives — caps us at roughly 1 GB of storage. That's nowhere near enough to host every meme this community has ever produced. So the Archive splits into two layers:

THE COMPLETE INDEX

A publicly searchable catalogue of every archived entry — title, author, tags, score, source post — whether or not we could host the file.

PUBLIC MEDIA

A curated selection of the actual files, prioritized by score, historical significance, and community relevance, hosted within our ~1 GB limit.

An entry that's "index only" isn't missing from the archive — it's fully catalogued, just not one of the files we could afford to host publicly yet. If you need the actual file, you can request it and an Archiver will look into it.

Who runs this

The Archive is maintained by volunteer Archivers from within r/MemeExchangeCommunism — the same community that elects its own mods rather than appointing them. Archivers catalogue new submissions, fix incomplete records, and decide which media makes the cut for public hosting.

Not affiliated, not corporate

Like the parent sub, the name borrows an aesthetic, not a politics. This is a hobby preservation project run by internet strangers who think memes are worth keeping around.
